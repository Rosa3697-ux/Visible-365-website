05 / ANSWERS

Good to know.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. We guide you through content, design, domain setup and launch.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Register it in your own name. We help configure it with your permission.

Can you create my logo and business cards?

Yes. We quote branding services separately so you only pay for what you need.

Can my website be bilingual?

Growth includes English and Spanish versions of five core pages. Essential includes one language.

Do you work with businesses nationwide?

Yes. We serve businesses in all 50 U.S. states. Consultations, design reviews and ongoing support can be handled remotely in English or Spanish.

What happens after launch?

We manage hosting and included edits. Additional work is quoted before it is performed.
