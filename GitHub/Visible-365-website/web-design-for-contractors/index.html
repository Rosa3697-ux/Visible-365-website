Frequently asked questions

What if I work with subcontractors?

Describe your coordination role accurately and avoid claiming that every trade is performed in-house.

Can I show residential and commercial projects?

Yes. Separate the galleries and contact paths so visitors can understand the type and size of work you take.

How can the site reduce unqualified inquiries?

Explain your minimum project scope and geographic coverage; use a short inquiry form with project type and timing.
